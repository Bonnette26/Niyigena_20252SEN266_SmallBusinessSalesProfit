## 1. Introduction

This document describes the data sources, data requirements, data collection methods, and data quality procedures for the **Small Business Sales and Profit Tracking System**.

The project aims to develop a database system using Oracle Database 26ai Free and PL/SQL to manage business information, record sales, track expenses, and calculate business profit.

The system will use secondary data from the UCI Machine Learning Repository and, where permission is obtained, supplementary information from a small business. Additional audit records will be generated automatically within the Oracle database.

The selected sources will support the development, testing, and evaluation of the database system.

## 2. Data Sources

The project will use the following three categories of data sources.

### 2.1 Secondary Data Source: UCI Online Retail Dataset

The main secondary data source is the **Online Retail Dataset**, obtained from the UCI Machine Learning Repository.

- **Dataset Name:** Online Retail
- **Author:** Daqing Chen
- **Repository:** UCI Machine Learning Repository
- **Year:** 2015
- **Dataset URL:** https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/352/online+retail
- **DOI:** https://doi.org/10.24432/C5BW33
- **License:** Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
- **Number of Records:** 541,909
- **Data Period:** 1 December 2010 to 9 December 2011
- **File Format:** Excel (.xlsx)

The dataset contains historical online retail transaction information. Its fields include invoice number, product code, product description, quantity, invoice date, unit price, customer ID, and country.

**Purpose of using this dataset:**

- To provide sample transaction records for database development and testing.
- To understand how retail sales information is structured.
- To support the development of sales and customer-related operations.
- To test sales calculations and reporting features.

**Limitation of the dataset:**

The dataset provides selling prices but does not provide all the purchase costs of products or the operating expenses of a small business. Therefore, it cannot independently provide all the information required to calculate actual business profit.

Additional cost and expense information will need to come from separately documented supplementary data.

### 2.2 Primary Data Source: Small Business Records

The project also plans to use relevant information from a small business, subject to obtaining permission from the business owner.

Potential information includes:

- Product names and descriptions.
- Product purchase costs.
- Product selling prices.
- Sales transaction records.
- Quantities of products sold.
- Business operating expenses.
- Supplier information.
- Relevant customer information.

**Purpose of using this source:**

- To understand real small-business sales and expense activities.
- To identify practical business data requirements.
- To support realistic database testing.
- To help ensure that the proposed system reflects common small-business operations.

**Data collection status:** This source is planned and will only be used if permission is granted. Until the information is obtained, sample or clearly labelled demonstration records will be used.

### 2.3 System-Generated Data Source: Oracle Audit Records

The system will generate audit records within Oracle Database using PL/SQL triggers or other appropriate database auditing procedures.

These records will help track selected activities performed in the database, such as inserting, updating, or deleting business records.

The audit information may include:

- Audit record ID.
- Table name.
- Operation performed.
- Record identifier.
- Date and time of the operation.
- Database user responsible for the operation.

**Purpose of using this source:**

- To monitor selected database activities.
- To maintain a history of important data changes.
- To improve accountability.
- To support database security and data integrity.

Audit records are generated by the system; they are not part of the external UCI dataset.

## 3. Data Requirements

The system requires the following categories of information:

| Data Category | Required Information | Purpose |

1. Product Data: Product ID, product name, description, purchase cost, selling price, and stock quantity : To manage products and calculate product-level profit 

2. Sales Data: Sale ID, sale date, customer ID, and total sale amount : To record and manage sales transactions 

3. Sale Details: Sale detail ID, sale ID, product ID, quantity, unit selling price, and unit cost at the time of sale : To record the individual products included in each sale 

4. Customer Data: Customer ID, customer name, contact information, and other necessary details : To identify and manage customers 

5. Supplier Data: Supplier ID, supplier name, contact information, and address : To manage suppliers 

6. User Data: User ID, username, and role : To identify authorized system users

7. Expense Data: Expense ID, expense category, amount, date, and description : To record business operating expenses 

8. Audit Data: Audit ID, table name, operation type, record ID, username, and timestamp : To track selected database activities 

Only information necessary for the project's functions will be collected and stored.

## 4. Database Tables and Data Organization

The project will organize the collected and generated information into eight main relational database tables.

| No. | Table Name | Description |

1. SUPPLIERS: Stores information about business suppliers. 
2. CUSTOMERS: Stores customer information. 
3. USERS: Stores information about users who access the system. 
4. PRODUCTS: Stores product details, purchase costs, selling prices, and stock quantities. 
5. SALES: Stores general information about each sales transaction. 
6. SALE_DETAILS: Stores the individual products and quantities included in each sale. 
7. EXPENSES: Stores business operating expenses. 
8. AUDIT_LOG: Stores audit records generated from selected database operations. 

### Relationships Between Tables

- One supplier can supply many products.
- One customer can have many sales.
- One user can be associated with multiple sales records.
- One sale can contain multiple sale details.
- One product can appear in multiple sale details.
- Audit records can track selected operations performed on the database tables.

Primary keys and foreign keys will be used to establish relationships and maintain referential integrity.

## 6. Data Quality and Validation

Data quality is important because incorrect or incomplete information can lead to inaccurate sales and profit reports.

The following procedures will be applied:

- **Completeness:** Check that required fields contain appropriate values.
- **Accuracy:** Verify that prices, quantities, dates, and expense amounts are reasonable.
- **Consistency:** Use consistent formats for dates, currency values, and other fields.
- **Uniqueness:** Use primary keys to prevent duplicate record identifiers.
- **Validity:** Apply constraints to ensure that values follow defined business rules.
- **Referential Integrity:** Use foreign keys to ensure that related records exist.
- **Data Cleaning:** Inspect the imported dataset for duplicate records, missing values, cancellations, and unusual quantities or prices before using it.

For example, the UCI dataset identifies invoices beginning with the letter C as cancellations. Such records must be examined and handled appropriately during data preparation rather than automatically treated as ordinary sales.

## 7. Data Privacy and Security

The project will apply appropriate measures to protect business and customer information.

The following measures will be considered:

- Obtain permission before using actual business records.
- Avoid collecting unnecessary personal information.
- Use sample or anonymized customer information during demonstrations.
- Restrict access to authorized database users.
- Apply appropriate database privileges.
- Maintain audit records for selected important operations.
- Avoid publishing confidential business or customer information in the public GitHub repository.

## 8. Data Limitations and Mitigation Strategies

| Limitation | Potential Impact | Mitigation Strategy |

| UCI dataset does not provide all product purchase costs | Complete product profit calculations cannot be obtained from the dataset alone | Use separately documented purchase-cost data or clearly labelled sample values |

| UCI dataset does not provide business operating expenses | Net profit cannot be calculated using the dataset alone | Add expense information from permitted business records or labelled demonstration data |

| Actual small-business records may not be available | The system may initially depend on sample data | Use the UCI dataset and clearly identified sample records |

| Dataset contains historical transactions | The records may not represent current business activities | Use the dataset mainly for development and testing |

| Dataset may contain cancellations or unusual records | Incorrect sales totals may result if records are not checked | Inspect and clean the data before importing it |

| Customer or business information may be confidential | Unauthorized disclosure may occur | Obtain permission and use anonymized or sample information where appropriate |

## 9. Expected Data Outputs and Calculations

The collected and organized data will support the following system outputs:

- Total sales revenue.
- Total quantity of products sold.
- Product sales performance.
- Total cost of goods sold.
- Total operating expenses.
- Gross profit.
- Net profit.
- Sales and profit trends.
- Audit activity reports.

The main calculations will be:

**Sales Revenue**

Sales Revenue = Quantity Sold × Unit Selling Price

**Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)**

COGS = Quantity Sold × Unit Cost at the Time of Sale

**Gross Profit**

Gross Profit = Sales Revenue − Cost of Goods Sold

**Net Profit**

Net Profit = Gross Profit − Operating Expenses

The system will store the unit selling price and unit cost at the time of each sale in SALE_DETAILS. This helps preserve historical transaction values even if product prices change later.
